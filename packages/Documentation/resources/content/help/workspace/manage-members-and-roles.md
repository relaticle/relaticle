Membership is managed on **Workspace Settings** — click your workspace name
at the top of the sidebar to reach it. Invitations, roles, and removals all
live there.

## The two roles

| | Administrator | Editor |
|---|---|---|
| Work with records — view, create, update | Yes | Yes |
| Delete and restore records | Yes | Yes |
| **Force delete** (permanent) | Yes | No |

Administrators can perform any action; Editors have the ability to read,
create, and update. Two things belong to the **workspace owner** alone,
whatever the role: the **Custom Fields** page and billing.

## Invitations

Send an invite from **Add Member** with an email address and a role. Until
it's accepted it sits under **Pending Invitations**, where you can:

- **Copy Link** — grab the invitation's accept URL to hand over directly, if
  the email didn't arrive.
- **Resend** the email.
- **Revoke** the invitation.

Invitations expire — the **Expires** column shows when.

![The Add Member form with the Administrator and Editor role choices, and a pending invitation with Copy Link, Resend, and Revoke actions](/help-assets/workspace/manage-members-and-roles-1.png)

There's also a workspace **join link**, offered on the invite step when you
[create a workspace](/help/workspace/rename-or-delete-your-workspace):
anyone with it joins as an Editor, and the link stays valid for 7 days.

## Changing and removing members

In the **Members** list, click a member's role to open **Manage Role** and
switch them between Administrator and Editor. **Remove** takes a member out
of the workspace — their records stay, they lose access. Members can also
**Leave** a workspace themselves, except the one they created.
