Your personal settings live under your avatar in the top-right corner —
choose **Settings**. They're yours alone and follow you across every
workspace you belong to.

## Profile information

Upload a profile photo (crop it in the editor, or **Remove photo** to go
back to initials), and change your name or email. Changing the email sends a
verification link to the new address — the change applies once you confirm
it.

## Password and sessions

**Update Password** needs your current password. If you signed up with a
social account and never set one, the same section offers **Set Password**.

**Browser Sessions** lists everywhere you're signed in — device, browser,
and last activity. **Log Out Other Browser Sessions** signs out every device
except the one you're using; do this whenever you've used a shared machine.

## Notifications

The **Notifications** page beside your profile settings controls task
assignment alerts and the daily digest — described in
[Work with tasks](/help/tasks-and-notes/work-with-tasks).

## Deleting your account

**Delete Account** schedules your account for deletion **30 days out**, not
immediately. Signing back in during those 30 days shows a single question —
**Keep my account** cancels the deletion on the spot. After the grace period
the account and its personal data are removed permanently.

If you own workspaces that still have other members, Relaticle asks you to
transfer their ownership before it will schedule the deletion — your team's
data doesn't disappear with you.
