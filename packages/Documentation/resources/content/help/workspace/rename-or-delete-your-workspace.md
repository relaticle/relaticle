A workspace is a self-contained CRM: its own records, custom fields, members,
and billing. Nothing is shared between workspaces, and you can belong to
several at once.

## Rename it

On **Workspace Settings** (click your workspace name in the sidebar), the
**Workspace Name** section holds the display name and the **Workspace Slug**
— the lowercase identifier in your workspace's URL. Renaming is safe any
time; changing the slug changes your URLs, so update any bookmarks.

![The Workspace Name section with the name and slug fields and their URL helper text](/help-assets/workspace/rename-or-delete-your-workspace-1.png)

## Run more than one

The workspace switcher at the top of the sidebar lists every workspace
you're a member of — switching is instant. **Create Workspace** in the same
menu starts a short wizard: name it, pick its URL handle, tell Relaticle
what you'll use it for, and invite your first teammates — by email or by
copying the 7-day join link.

Separate clients, separate businesses, or a sandbox to try things in — each
workspace keeps its data, fields, and plan entirely to itself.

## Delete it

At the bottom of Workspace Settings, **Delete Workspace** winds a workspace
down with a safety net: deletion is scheduled **30 days out**, and the page
shows a **Cancel Deletion** button for the whole grace period. After the 30
days, the workspace and all its records are permanently removed —
[export your data](/help/records/export-your-data) first if you'll want it.

Your original personal workspace can't be deleted, and only the workspace
owner sees the delete section.
