Custom attributes capture anything Relaticle doesn't track out of the box, such
as a lead source, a renewal date, or an internal priority, on companies, people,
opportunities, notes, or tasks. Add one from the Custom attributes page and it
appears on that entity's create and edit forms right away, with no other
setup.

1. Click your workspace name at the top of the sidebar, then click
   **Custom attributes**.
2. Select the entity tab: **Companies**, **People**, **Opportunities**,
   **Notes**, or **Tasks**.
3. Click **Add attribute**.
4. Choose a **Type**: Text, Number, Currency, Date, Select, Multi Select,
   Checkbox, Link, Tags Input, and more.
5. Type a name into **Name**.
6. For Select-type attributes, click **Add Option** for each choice.
7. Click **Submit**.

![The Custom attributes page for Opportunities, listing Amount, Close Date, and Stage as System attributes alongside a Deal Source attribute](/help-assets/getting-started/use-custom-fields-1.png)

The attribute shows up immediately wherever that entity is created or edited.

![The Create Opportunity form with a new Deal Source attribute appearing right below Stage](/help-assets/getting-started/use-custom-fields-2.png)

## Attribute settings

Each attribute has toggles for **Visible in List**, **Visible in View**,
**Toggleable Hidden**, and **Encrypted**, plus, for
single-choice attributes, **Enable Color Options** or **Unique Per Entity Type**.
Most attributes can also be filtered from the filter row above the entity's
list. See
[Find anything with search and filters](/help/getting-started/find-anything-with-search-and-filters).

## If an attribute is marked System, then you can edit it but not delete it

Attributes like Amount, Close Date, and Stage on opportunities, or Domains on
companies, ship with Relaticle and carry a **System** badge. Open one from
its **Actions** menu to rename it, or to reorder, recolor, or add its
options. The menu just won't offer **Delete**.
