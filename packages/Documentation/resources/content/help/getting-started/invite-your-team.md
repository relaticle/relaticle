Bring your team into the workspace so everyone works from the same records.
Send one or more invites by email and choose what they can do: manage the
workspace, edit records, or just view them.

1. Click your workspace name at the top of the sidebar, then click
   **Workspace Settings**.
2. Click the **Members** tab, then click **Invite team members**.
3. Type the addresses into **Send invite to**, separated by a comma, a
   space, or a new line. You can send up to 10 in one go.
4. Choose an **Invite as** role: **Administrator**, **Editor**, or
   **Viewer**. Everyone in this batch joins with that role.
5. Click **Send invitations**.

![The Invite team members dialog with two addresses in Send invite to and a role chosen in Invite as](/help-assets/getting-started/invite-your-team-1.png)

Each person receives an invitation email and appears in the **Members**
list marked **Invite pending** until they accept. The actions menu on that
row can **Resend** or **Revoke** the invite.

## If you invited the wrong address, then revoke it

Find the row marked **Invite pending** in **Members** and click **Revoke**
in its actions menu. It can no longer be used to join your workspace, and
you can send a new one with the correct address.
