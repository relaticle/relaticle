Bring your team into the workspace so everyone works from the same records.
Send one or more invites by email and choose what each person can do:
manage the workspace, edit records, or just view them.

1. Click your workspace name at the top of the sidebar, then click
   **Workspace Settings**.
2. Click the **Members** tab.
3. Click **Invite people**.
4. Type an address into **Email** and choose a role — **Administrator**,
   **Editor**, or **Viewer**. Click **Add another** to queue up more
   people, up to 10 in one go.
5. Click **Submit**.

![The Invite people modal with an email address and a role for each person being invited](/help-assets/getting-started/invite-your-team-1.png)

Each person receives an invitation email and appears in the workspace's
**People** list with an **Invitation pending** status until they accept.
From there you can **Copy Link**, **Resend**, or **Revoke** the invite.

## If you invited the wrong address, then revoke it

Find the invitation in the **People** list and click **Revoke**. It can no
longer be used to join your workspace, and you can send a new one with the
correct address.
