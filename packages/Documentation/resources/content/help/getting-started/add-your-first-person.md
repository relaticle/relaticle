A person record is someone you're in touch with — a contact at a company you
sell to, a candidate, or anyone else you want to track. You only need a name
to create one.

1. Click **People** in the sidebar.
2. Click **New person** in the top right.
3. Type their name into **Name** — this is the only required field.
4. Select a **Company** to link them to, or click the **+** next to it to
   create the company on the spot.
5. Fill in **Emails**, **Phone Number**, **Job Title**, or **LinkedIn** if you
   have them — you can add these later.
6. Click **Create**.

The person appears in your People list right away, and on the linked
company's **People** tab if you assigned one.

## If you don't see the fields you expect

Your team may have added or removed fields for people. Ask a workspace
admin — fields configured on the **Custom Fields** page show up on the create
form automatically.
