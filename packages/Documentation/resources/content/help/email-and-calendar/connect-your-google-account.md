Connecting a Google account brings your email and calendar into Relaticle.
Emails and meetings land on the records they involve, and you can send from
Relaticle with that address. For an Outlook or Microsoft 365 mailbox, see
[Connect your Microsoft account](/help/email-and-calendar/connect-your-microsoft-account).

## Connect it

1. Open the workspace menu at the top of the sidebar and choose
   **Workspace Settings**.
2. Open the **Email and Calendar** tab. It opens on **Accounts**.
3. Click **Connect Google account**.
4. Choose the account at Google and approve the access it lists.
5. Google sends you back to Relaticle, and the account appears under
   **Connected accounts**.

The **Emails** page and the **Meetings** panel on **Home** show the same
button until you connect an account.

![The Accounts tab under Email and Calendar, showing one connected Google account marked Default and In sync above the Connect Google account and Connect Microsoft account buttons](/help-assets/email-and-calendar/connect-your-google-account-1.png)

## What Relaticle asks Google for

- **Read your email**, so messages can appear on your records.
- **Send email**, so what you write in Relaticle goes out from your address.
- **See and respond to calendar events**, so meetings appear and your RSVP
  reaches your calendar.

Relaticle never changes, labels, or deletes messages in your mailbox, and it
never imports your Gmail drafts.

## What happens after you connect

The account belongs to the workspace you connected it in. Relaticle imports
your mailbox and calendar in the background, and you can keep working while
it runs. The account shows **Syncing** with a percentage during the import
and **In sync** once it has caught up.

New email and events keep arriving after that. What your workspace sees of
each email depends on the mailbox's sharing level, which starts at the
workspace default. [Choose who sees your email](/help/email-and-calendar/choose-who-sees-your-email)
before you connect a mailbox you consider sensitive.

## Manage a connected account

Open the menu on the account's row. The menu is the same for a Google and a
Microsoft account.

- **Manage** opens the account's settings. **General** holds **Sync inbox**,
  **Sync sent**, and the hourly and daily send limits. **Sharing**,
  **Mailbox-only blocklist**, and **Signatures** have a tab each.
- **Set as default** makes this the account the composer sends from when you
  have more than one.
- **Disconnect account** stops syncing, deletes the access tokens Relaticle
  holds, and removes the account's signatures.

## If the account shows Reconnect needed

Google has stopped accepting the saved access. Open the account's menu and
click **Reconnect**, then approve access again.
