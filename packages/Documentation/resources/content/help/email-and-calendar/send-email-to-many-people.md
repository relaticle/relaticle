Mass sending writes one message and sends it as a separate email to each
recipient. Nobody sees the other recipients, and merge tags fill in each
person's own details.

## Send to a selection

1. Open the **People** or **Companies** list.
2. Tick the records you want to reach.
3. Open the bulk actions menu and click **Send email**.
4. The composer opens as **New mass email** with the recipients listed.
   **Add recipients** adds more.
5. Write the subject and the message. **Insert variable** adds a merge tag
   such as full name or company.
6. Click **Send emails**. The button shows how many will go out.

Selected records without an email address are skipped, and Relaticle tells
you how many.

You can also start from any new email. Turn on **Mass sending** in the
composer and add recipients there.

## Reuse a message with a template

Write the message once, then click **Use template** and **Save as template**
in the composer. Tick **Share with my workspace** to let everyone use it.

Templates are listed under **Emails** in the **Templates** tab. Click
**New template** to write one from scratch.

## How fast a mass email goes out

Each connected account has an **Hourly send limit** and a **Daily send
limit**. A mass email joins your outbox and goes out as those limits allow,
so a large send can take hours.

- Watch progress on the **Emails** page under **Outbox**.
- Cancel what has not gone yet with **Cancel selected**.
- Change the limits in the account's settings, under **General**. Click the
  account in **Workspace Settings**, **Email and Calendar** to open them.
  Leave a limit blank to use the default.

## If the notice says No valid recipients

None of the selected people has an email address. Add an address to their
**Emails** field and select them again.
