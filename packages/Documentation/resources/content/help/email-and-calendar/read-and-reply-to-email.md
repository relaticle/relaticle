Synced email lives on the record it involves. Open a person, company, or
opportunity and click its **Emails** tab to read the conversation and
answer it. Relaticle matches each email to people by email address and to
companies by domain.

## Read a conversation

Each row shows the subject, the participants, the first line, and whose
mailbox it came through, as **via** and a name. Use **Search emails…** to
narrow the list.

Click a row to open the email. The reader shows the sender, the recipients,
the body, and any attachments.

![A person's Emails tab listing four conversations, two fully visible and two from a teammate's mailbox with a Request access button](/help-assets/email-and-calendar/read-and-reply-to-email-1.png)

A row from a teammate's mailbox shows only what that teammate shares. A
hidden subject reads **(subject hidden)**. See
[Request access to an email from a teammate](/help/email-and-calendar/request-access-to-an-email).

## Reply or forward

1. Open the email.
2. Click **Reply**, **Reply all**, or **Forward**.
3. Write your message and click **Send email**.

The reader also has **Sharing**, which sets who can see that one email, and
**Summarize thread**, which writes an AI summary of the conversation.

## Write a new email

Click **Compose** on the **Emails** tab, or **Compose email** at the top of
the record. The record's address is already in **To**.

- **From** picks the connected account to send from.
- **CC** and **BCC** add their fields.
- The toolbar formats text, and **Insert variable** adds a merge tag: full
  name, company, phone number, job title, LinkedIn, or today's date.
- **Attach files**, **Signature**, and **Use template** sit below the
  message.

Close a message before sending and it is saved as a draft.

## Add a signature

In the composer, click **Signature**, then **New signature**. Give it a
name, write the content, and tick **Use as my default signature** to add it
to every email from that account. You can also manage signatures from the
account's **Signatures** tab under **Workspace Settings**.

## Follow what you sent

The **Emails** page in the sidebar tracks your own outgoing mail in three
tabs.

- **Drafts**: messages you closed before sending. **Continue writing**
  reopens one.
- **Outbox**: email waiting to go, split into **Queued**, **Scheduled**,
  **Sending**, **Failed**, and **Sent in the last 24 hours**. You can cancel
  a queued email or change its **Send at** time. Pick **Failed** to see
  email that could not be delivered, with the reason, and click **Retry**.
- **Templates**: messages you saved to reuse.

## If Relaticle can't send from your account

The composer shows **Relaticle can't send from this account yet** when the
account was connected without send access. Click **Grant permission** and
approve it at Google or Microsoft.
