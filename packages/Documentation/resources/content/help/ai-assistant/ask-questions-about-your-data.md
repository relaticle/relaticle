The assistant reads your workspace's companies, people, opportunities, tasks,
and notes, so you can ask questions instead of clicking through lists —
"which deals close this month?", "summarise everything we know about Acme",
"what's the total pipeline value by stage?".

There are three ways to start:

- The **Home** page is a chat — type into **Ask anything...** and send.
- Click **Ask Relaticle** in the top bar to open the chat panel over whatever
  page you're on.
- Pick any past conversation from **Chats** in the sidebar, or press `n`
  for a new one.

![A chat conversation answering "which deals are closing this month" with a deal list and a pipeline-value-by-stage table](/help-assets/ai-assistant/ask-questions-about-your-data-1.png)

## Point it at specific records

Type **@** in the message box to mention a record — people, companies,
opportunities, tasks, and notes all come up as you type. A mentioned record is
passed to the assistant directly, so "what's the status of @Q1 Enterprise
Deal?" needs no further explanation.

When you open the chat panel from a record's page, the assistant already knows
which record you're looking at — a small context chip above the message box
shows it, and you can dismiss the chip if you don't want that context.

## What it can answer

- **Finding things** — it searches records the same way you can, and links
  each record it mentions.
- **Summaries** — a digest of a company and everything attached to it, or a
  workspace-wide overview.
- **Pipeline maths** — counts and totals grouped by stage or company, straight
  from your opportunity data.
- **Your team** — who's in the workspace, for assigning work.

For anything it can't do in chat — managing custom field definitions in bulk,
importing files, inviting teammates — it links you to the right page instead
of guessing.

## Manage your conversations

Every chat is saved. In the sidebar's **Chats** group you can rename or delete
a conversation from its row; **All chats** opens a searchable list of every
conversation. Titles are generated automatically from the first exchange.

Each answer has controls underneath: copy it, regenerate it, or rate it with
a thumbs up or down so the team knows how the assistant is doing. You can also
edit a message you already sent — the conversation replays from that point.
