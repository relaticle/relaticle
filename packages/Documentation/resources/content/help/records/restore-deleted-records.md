Deleting a company, person, opportunity, task, or note doesn't destroy it.
The record is moved out of sight and can be restored. Only **Force delete**,
reserved for the workspace owner and Admins, removes data permanently.

## Restore a record

1. Open the record type's list, say **Companies**.
2. Click **Filter** and pick **Deleted records** at the bottom of the field list.
   Click the second part of the chip and choose **only**.
3. Tick the record, then click **Restore selected** in the bar at the bottom
   of the list.

The record returns with everything intact: field values, linked tasks and
notes, activity history. Tick several rows to restore them together.

## Delete permanently

With the deleted-records filter active, the workspace owner and Admins also
see **Force delete selected** under **More** in that bar. That removes the record for good. There is no undo, and
no way for Relaticle support to bring it back. If there's any doubt,
[export the records first](/help/records/export-your-data).

## Good to know

- A merged record is not in the trash and cannot be restored. A merge is final, see
  [merge duplicate records](/help/records/merge-duplicate-records).
- A deleted record's page shows **This record was deleted** and a **Go back**
  button, so restoring starts from the list's trash filter. Deleted rows in the
  filter open nothing when clicked. Tick them and use **Restore selected**.
- Deleted records don't appear in lists, search, the board, or the AI
  assistant's answers.
- Deleting a company doesn't delete the people, tasks, or notes attached to
  it. Restoring the company shows them on its page again.
- Everyone but Viewers can delete and restore; only the owner and Admins can
  force delete.
