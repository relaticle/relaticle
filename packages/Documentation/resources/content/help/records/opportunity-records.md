An opportunity is a deal in progress: what it's worth, when it should close,
which stage it's in, and who it's with. Opportunities are the records behind
your pipeline board.

## The fields

- **Name**: required.
- **Company** and **Point of Contact**: the company and the person you're working
  with, both linked on the record page.
- **Amount**: the deal's value, shown as a badge on board cards by default.
- **Close Date**: drives the board's "Closes Today" / "Overdue" badges.
- **Stage**: a select whose options *are* your pipeline;
  [rename or reorder the stages](/help/custom-fields/edit-the-options-in-a-select-field)
  to match your sales process.
- Plus your custom attributes for opportunities.

## List and board

The **Opportunities** page has a view switcher next to the heading. **All
opportunities** is the full sortable table. **Pipeline** is the board. It groups
deals into a column per stage by default, and it is the view the page opens on until you
move another view to the top of the switcher. Dragging a card is how you
[move a deal forward](/help/getting-started/track-a-deal-through-the-pipeline).
Click **Filter** above the board to narrow it by most attributes. Click **Sort**
to order the cards in every column. Without a sort, cards stay in the order you
dragged them into.

Open **View settings** to shape the board. Under **Grouped by**, pick another
single-choice attribute to make the columns. Under **Visible columns**, switch a
column off to hide it. Drag a column header, or drag the list in **Visible
columns**, to put the columns in your own order. Switch on **No Stage** to add a first column for deals with no stage.
Under **Lanes**, pick **Company** to split the board into a lane per company. While lanes
are shown, the bar offers no **Sort** and cards stay in hand order.

Under **Visible attributes**, choose what each card shows, reorder it, and add
more with **Add attribute to view**. **Show attribute labels** and **Hide empty
attributes** adjust how the card reads. The two buttons at the end of the
filter row switch the columns between narrow and wide and the cards between
compact and full. The board remembers these settings, your filters and your
sort for you alone, and the stage field's settings stay as they are. Choose
**Create new view** in the view switcher to save the board as a view only you see.

The **New opportunity** button in the header creates a deal in the first visible
column. Every column also has its own add button for creating a deal directly
in that stage. Hover a card and click its preview button to open the deal in a side panel without
leaving the board. In the list, the same button sits beside the name.

## The record page

An opportunity's page links its company and point of contact and lists every
visible field. It opens on **Overview**: the stage, amount, and close date up
top, then the latest activity, notes, and open tasks. **Activity**, **Tasks**,
and **Notes** tabs follow. Tasks and notes can be created here pre-linked, or
attached if they already exist.

With email and calendar sync on, **Emails** and **Meetings** tabs appear
here too.
