An opportunity is a deal in progress: what it's worth, when it should close,
which stage it's in, and who it's with. Opportunities are the records behind
your pipeline board.

## The fields

- **Name**: required.
- **Company** and **Point of Contact**: the company and the person you're working
  with, both linked on the record page.
- **Amount**: the deal's value, shown as a badge on board cards.
- **Close Date**: drives the board's "Closes Today" / "Overdue" badges.
- **Stage**: a select whose options *are* your pipeline;
  [rename or reorder the stages](/help/custom-fields/edit-the-options-in-a-select-field)
  to match your sales process.
- Plus your custom fields for opportunities.

## List and board

The **Opportunities** page has a **List / Board** switcher next to the
heading. The list is the full sortable table; the board groups deals into a
column per stage, and dragging a card is how you
[move a deal forward](/help/getting-started/track-a-deal-through-the-pipeline).
The board can be filtered by **Company** and **Point of Contact**, and every column
has its own add button for creating a deal directly in that stage. Hover a
card and click its preview button to open the deal in a side panel without
leaving the board. In the list, the same button sits beside the name, and
**Open preview** is in the row menu.

## The record page

An opportunity's page links its company and point of contact and lists every
visible field. It opens on **Overview**: the stage, amount, and close date up
top, then the latest activity, notes, and open tasks. **Activity**, **Tasks**,
and **Notes** tabs follow. Tasks and notes can be created here pre-linked, or
attached if they already exist.

With email and calendar sync on, **Emails** and **Meetings** tabs appear
here too.
