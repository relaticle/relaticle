The task board is the same tasks as the list, laid out as a kanban: one
column per status, a card per task. To open it, use the view menu at the
top left of the Tasks page, which shows **All tasks**, and choose **Board**.

## Reading the board

By default, each card shows the task's title, a snippet of its description, a flag badge
for **Priority**, a **Due Date** badge that turns into "Due Today",
"Due Tomorrow", or "(Overdue)" as the date approaches, and the assignees'
avatars. Choose what a card shows under **Visible attributes** in **View settings**.

Click **Filter** to narrow the whole board by most attributes. Filter by **Assignees** to see one person's plate. Click **Sort** to order the cards in every column. Open **View settings** and pick **Assignee** under **Lanes** to split the board into a lane per assignee. A **Sort** orders the cards inside each lane too.

Without a sort, cards keep the order you dragged them into. With a sort set, you can still drag a card to another column but not reorder it by hand. The two buttons at the end of the filter row switch the columns between narrow and wide and the cards between compact and full.

![The task board with To do, In progress, and Done columns, cards showing priority flags, due-date badges, and assignee avatars](/help-assets/tasks-and-notes/use-the-task-board-1.png)

## Working the board

- **Drag a card** to another column to change its status. The change saves
  as it lands.
- **Add a task in place** with the **+** button at the top of any column; the
  status is pre-set to that column. **New task** in the header starts in the
  first visible column.
- **Edit or delete** a card from the menu on the card itself.

## Reorder the columns for yourself

Drag a column header left or right to move that status. You can also click
**View settings** at the top of the board and drag the statuses under **Visible
columns**, or use **Move up** and **Move down** from a status's menu. Switch a
status off there to hide its column. Switch on **No Status** to add a first column
for tasks with no status. The order is saved
for you alone, so teammates keep theirs. The board also remembers your filters,
sort and lanes. Choose **Create new view** in the view switcher to save it as a view only you see. It leaves the **Status** field's own
order untouched, and a status added later joins the end of your order.

## The columns are yours

Columns mirror the task **Status** field's options unless you group the board
by another attribute under **Grouped by**. The options are To do, In progress, and
Done out of the box. The workspace owner or an Admin can rename them, add
more, or reorder them on the Custom attributes page. That order is where every
member's board starts:
[edit the options in a select attribute](/help/custom-fields/edit-the-options-in-a-select-field).
